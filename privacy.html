Privacy Policy

This policy covers ai-coproducer.com, the company website of AI Co-Producer LLC, a North Carolina limited liability company at 4030 Wake Forest Road, Ste 349, Raleigh, NC 27609.

What this website collects

Nothing about you. The website has no sign-in, no forms, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking cookies. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, to run and protect the site. We do not use those logs to identify or profile visitors.

When you email us

If you write to support@ai-coproducer.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to send. We use it only to answer you and keep a record of the conversation. We never sell it or share it for marketing.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to have an email conversation deleted, write to support@ai-coproducer.com.
